The full story

Entrepreneur. Journalist. Interviewer. Storyteller.

Sarvesh Mishra is an Indian entrepreneur, journalist, interviewer, author, and storyteller. His career spans nearly two decades — from ground-level journalism across 11 Indian states to building media companies and conscious-living platforms.

This is the official profile of Sarvesh Mishra, Founder of Red Hot Media House Pvt. Ltd. and CureSoulLife Pvt. Ltd., and host of The Sarvesh Mishra Show — not to be confused with other individuals of the same name.

Sarvesh began working at a young age — not from theory, but from responsibility, struggle, and direct observation. He spent nearly 15 years in the television and media industry, reporting and working on the ground across India. From the Himalayas to Kashmir, from Bihar to Maharashtra, his on-ground experience gave him a perspective on India's social, psychological, and human realities that no studio or classroom could replicate.

This journalism foundation — which included covering the 2013 Uttarakhand disaster — shaped everything that followed. The discipline of asking questions, listening deeply, and finding truth beneath the surface became the lens through which Sarvesh now builds media, interviews leaders, and develops intellectual property.

Over the years, his work expanded from media into entrepreneurship. He founded Red Hot Media House Pvt. Ltd., a media and production company focused on long-form content, podcasts, and IP development. He later founded CureSoulLife Pvt. Ltd., a conscious living and inner transformation platform envisioned to work globally.

Sarvesh Mishra is also the author of a novel, reflecting his long-standing engagement with storytelling, human emotions, and inner conflict.

Today, he actively runs and hosts three long-form digital media properties:

  • The Sarvesh Mishra Show — In-depth podcast conversations with entrepreneurs, leaders, authors, and thinkers on leadership, business, psychology, and human development.
  • The Inner Wealth — A structured 7-day deep-dive series where a single guest explores one inner challenge across seven consecutive episodes.
  • The Urban Sannyasi — A platform exploring human values, spirituality, consciousness, and ancient wisdom decoded for modern urban life.

His work bridges journalism and entrepreneurship, depth and scale, business acumen and human understanding. Whether he is interviewing a guest, building a media brand, or developing a wellness platform, the same principle applies: depth over noise, substance over spectacle.

Sarvesh Mishra does not position himself as a guru or motivational speaker. He is a journalist who became an entrepreneur — building from ground-level truth, not from theory.
