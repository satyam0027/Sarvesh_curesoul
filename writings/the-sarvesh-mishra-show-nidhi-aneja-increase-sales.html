Most businesses do not have a traffic problem. They have a conversation problem. People visit, call, message and ask for prices, and then quietly disappear because nobody guided them to a decision. If your sales have flattened, your team keeps missing targets or you are starting from zero, the fix is rarely a bigger advertising budget. It is a better sales process.

That is the idea behind this long conversation between Sarvesh Mishra and sales expert Nidhi Aneja on The Sarvesh Mishra Show. For the series itself, start at What Is The Sarvesh Mishra Show?

Why sales stop growing

  • The offer is unclear. If a customer cannot repeat what you do in one sentence, they will not recommend or buy it.
  • There is no follow up. Many buyers need more than one conversation. Silence after the first call loses the sale.
  • The wrong people are in the funnel. More leads do not help if they cannot afford or do not need your product.
  • Nothing is measured. Without numbers, every month feels like luck.

सेल बढ़ाने के लिए क्या करना चाहिए? Five practical steps

These are the questions people search most on Google right now, so here is a plain answer you can use today. Keep the steps that match what Nidhi teaches.

  • Know exactly who you are selling to. Write down who buys, what problem they have and what they already tried.
  • Make your offer easy to understand. One sentence on who it is for and what changes for them.
  • Follow up on a schedule. Decide before the first conversation ends when and how you will follow up.
  • Ask for feedback and referrals. Happy customers are your cheapest source of new customers.
  • Track a few numbers. Enquiries, conversations and closed sales are enough to start.

How to answer common customer objections

What the customer saysWhat may be behind itA calm way to respond
“It is too expensive.”They do not yet see the valueAsk what result they expect, then connect the price to that result
“I will think about it.”A doubt they have not said aloudAsk, “What would you like to be sure about before deciding?”
“Send me the details.”Polite way to end the callSend a short summary and agree a time to talk again
“I already work with someone.”Fear of switchingAsk what is working and what they would improve

A seven day plan to improve your sales

One week, one process

Do not overhaul everything. Write the offer, find where people drop, then follow up the leads you already have.

  • Day 1: Write your offer in one sentence.
  • Day 2: List your last 20 enquiries and mark which became customers.
  • Day 3: Draw your funnel and find the stage where most people drop.
  • Day 4: Write your follow up message and objection answers.
  • Day 5: Contact every enquiry from the past month who never bought.
  • Day 6: Ask three happy customers for feedback and one introduction each.
  • Day 7: Review your numbers and pick one change for next week.

Frequently asked questions

How can I increase sales in my business?
Understand your customer, make your offer clear, follow up consistently, ask for referrals and track your numbers.
What is a good sales formula for beginners?
Start with a simple loop: find the right customer, explain the value, follow up, close and ask for feedback.
How do I do sales marketing?
Find where your best customers come from and put your effort there. Use content, referrals and direct outreach together.
Why are my sales not growing?
Common causes are an unclear offer, no follow up, no tracking and targeting the wrong customer.
How do I handle the objection that my price is too high?
Ask what result the customer expects, then connect your price to that result instead of defending the number.
How often should I follow up with a lead?
There is no single rule, but agree the next step at the end of every conversation and follow up within a day.
What are the most common mistakes that slow down sales?
Talking more than listening, leading with price, not qualifying leads, relying on a fixed script, skipping preparation, ignoring existing customers and not following up.
What is the 30 60 90 rule in sales?
It usually means a 90 day plan for a new salesperson: learn the product and customers in the first 30 days, start selling and build a pipeline by day 60, and own targets by day 90. Some teams use the name slightly differently, so check the version your company follows.
What is the 3 3 3 rule in sales?
There is no single official definition. Different trainers use the name for different routines, so check which version a source means before you copy it.
What are the 7 golden rules of sales?
There is no official list. A practical set is: know your customer, listen more than you talk, explain value before price, qualify your leads, follow up consistently, handle objections calmly, and ask for feedback and referrals.
What are the 7 C's in sales?
Trainers use different lists under this name, so the label alone does not tell you which framework is meant. The common thread is customer focus, clear communication and trust.
What are common sales problems and solutions?
Low enquiries call for better visibility, low conversion calls for a clearer offer and objection answers, and low repeat sales call for follow up and feedback after delivery.
Can anyone learn to sell?
Yes. Selling is a skill built through practice, feedback and a repeatable process.
How long does it take to see better sales?
It depends on your product and market. Most people notice a change once they follow up consistently and track results for a few weeks.

The practical thread is process, not louder ads: a one-sentence offer, a next step before you hang up, and three numbers you actually look at. Watch the episode for Nidhi Aneja’s answer in her own words.
