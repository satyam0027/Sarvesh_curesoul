When markets shake, almost everyone asks the same question: where is the safe place for my money? For generations in India, the answer has been gold. On The Sarvesh Mishra Show, Sarvesh Mishra sits with Jasmiit Siingh to test a bold claim: that only those who hold gold will survive.

Quick answer: Gold has often held up in global crises, but it is not guaranteed to rise when markets fall. Reports show it can drop sharply when investors need cash. A balanced plan uses gold as one part of your savings, not all of it.

What is the global crisis alert everyone is talking about?

Several warnings are being discussed together, according to publications in the Google results:

  • Rising global debt. Yahoo Finance reported in October 2026 that global debt reached 365.5 trillion dollars in the first half of 2026.
  • Debt and bond yields. ITM Trading wrote on 1 October 2026 that the global debt crisis is pushing Treasury yields higher, and asked what that means for gold.
  • Central banks and gold. Fortune reported on 18 October 2026 that central banks around the world have been reshuffling their gold holdings while also adding to them.

These are headlines from different sources, not a single forecast. Together they explain why gold is in the spotlight.

Why gold is called a safe haven

Gold earns its reputation for a few reasons:

  • No issuer. Unlike a bond or a bank balance, gold does not depend on anyone’s promise to pay.
  • Limited supply. New gold is hard to produce, so it cannot be printed like currency.
  • Central bank demand. Many central banks hold gold as part of their reserves.
  • Long history. People have valued it through wars, inflation and currency crises.

The World Gold Council’s weekly markets monitor on 2 March 2026 reported that gold returns were positive about 64 percent of the time in the weeks after a crisis spike, compared with 36 percent for oil. That is a record of tendencies, not a promise.

Publishers describe the same idea in similar words. Muthoot Finance (20 January 2026) points to gold’s strong liquidity and its role as a hedge against inflation, free from geopolitical risk and currency devaluation. Yahoo Finance (20 October 2026) says gold is usually a safe haven because it has historically held its value, but adds that its price can be volatile in the short term. VanEck describes gold as having outperformed stocks and bonds in crises, with resilience and low correlation. An academic paper by D. G. Baur argues that the decision to buy gold is rooted in behavioural biases tied to its history as a currency, a store of value and a safe haven.

Is gold an asset or a commodity?

It is both. Gold is a commodity because it is a physical metal that trades on markets like oil or silver. It is an asset because owning it is part of your savings and it can store value. Gold you own is an asset. A loan taken against your gold is a liability. Unlike a share or a bond, gold does not pay you dividends or interest, so any gain depends only on its price rising.

Why is gold falling today in India?

This is one of the most searched questions, and there is no single answer. Common reasons that analysts discuss include:

  • Cash needs. Investors sell winners, including gold, to cover losses elsewhere.
  • Higher interest rates and yields. Gold pays no interest, so rising yields can make it less attractive.
  • A stronger dollar. Gold is priced in dollars, so a stronger dollar can weigh on it.
  • Profit booking. After a big rise, some investors sell to lock in gains.

Prices move every day, so check a live rate and a reliable news source before you decide anything.

Gold prices in India also depend on the rupee exchange rate and local taxes, so a dollar forecast does not translate one for one into a rupee price.

What “Gold Wala Hi Bachega” means in practice

Read as a warning against having nothing but cash and shares in a crisis, the idea makes sense. Read as “put everything into gold”, it does not. A sensible version looks like this:

  1. Keep an emergency fund first. Hold several months of expenses in cash or a bank account.
  2. Hold gold as one part of your savings. Decide a modest share based on your goals and speak to an adviser.
  3. Buy in parts. Spreading purchases over time reduces the risk of buying at a peak.
  4. Do not borrow to buy gold. Interest costs can wipe out any gain.
  5. Check purity and costs. For jewellery and coins, look for a hallmark and ask about making charges.
  6. Know your options. Jewellery, coins and bars, gold ETFs and gold funds all have different costs and rules, so compare them.
  7. Review once or twice a year. Adjust your mix as your life and the market change.
Key takeaways
  • Gold has often held up in crises, but it is not guaranteed to rise.
  • It can fall when investors sell to raise cash, as reports showed in early 2026.
  • Gold is both a commodity and an asset. A loan against gold is a liability.
  • 2027 forecasts disagree by about 1,800 dollars an ounce. Nobody knows.
  • Use gold as one part of savings, not all of it. Do not borrow to buy gold.

Frequently asked questions

Is gold safe in a global crisis?

Gold has often held up in crises, but it can also fall when investors sell to raise cash. It is safer as part of a mix than as your only holding.

Why is gold price falling today in India?

Common reasons are selling to raise cash, higher interest rates and yields, a stronger dollar and profit booking. Check a live source for today’s reason.

Is gold price going down in India?

Prices move daily and no one can predict them. Check the current rate and recent trend before you buy or sell.

Will gold rise in a global crisis?

It often has, according to long term data, but the response is not immediate or guaranteed. Reports show gold fell sharply in early 2026.

What is gold crash today?

It usually refers to a sharp one day or short term fall in the price. Use live news for the latest, and avoid making panic decisions.

What is a global crisis alert?

It is a warning, from media or analysts, about risks such as rising debt, falling markets or tension between countries. It is not a prediction of a specific event.

How much of my savings should I hold in gold?

There is no single rule. Many advisers suggest a modest share depending on your goals, so speak to a qualified adviser.

What is the gold rate today?

The rate changes through the day and differs by city and source, so check a live rate from a trusted site or your jeweller.

Will gold prices crash in 2027?

No one can say. Analysts disagree, with one forecast reported by CNBC TV18 seeing gold lower by October 2027 and another reported by DIY Investor seeing it higher. Plan for both outcomes.

Will gold hit 6000 in 2027?

If you mean 6,000 dollars per ounce, some forecasts, such as J.P. Morgan’s reported expectation of about 6,300 dollars by the end of 2027, are above that level, while others are well below it. It is possible but not certain.

Will gold go down in the next 5 years?

Gold has had long periods of rising and falling prices. Nobody can predict the next five years, so avoid putting all your savings in one asset.

What did Warren Buffett say about gold?

Buffett has long been a critic of gold as an investment, arguing that it produces nothing and preferring productive businesses. Read his original shareholder letters for his exact words.

Is gold an asset or a commodity?

Both. It is a physical commodity traded on markets, and an asset when you own it as part of your savings. A loan against gold is a liability.

Is gold really a safe haven?

It has often held its value in crises, but its price can be very volatile in the short term, as the early 2026 fall showed.

What will the gold price be in 2027 in Indian rupees?

No one can say. It depends on the dollar price of gold, the rupee exchange rate and taxes, so any rupee forecast is a guess.
