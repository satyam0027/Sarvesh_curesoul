Can Yoga Be a Career? Yogacharya Dhakaram on Yoga Teacher Training, Business and the Future of Yoga

Guest: Yogacharya Dhakaram — yoga teacher, trainer & wellness educator
Host: Sarvesh Mishra · @sarveshmishrashow

Watch: Yoga में दौलत और शोहरत दोनों! Yoga का Career, Business और Future क्या है? | Yogacharya Dhakaram

Is yoga only an ancient wellness practice, or can it become a livelihood — a career, a studio, even a business? On this episode of The Sarvesh Mishra Show, host Sarvesh Mishra sits down with Yogacharya Dhakaram to talk teacher training, certificates versus real teaching ability, and the future of yoga as a profession. Can Yoga Really Become a Career in India?

According to Yogacharya Dhakaram, yoga can provide a professional path when it is approached with seriousness, discipline and continuous learning. During the conversation, he describes yoga as an option for livelihood and explains the importance of structured teacher training.

He discusses a training model that includes a 250-hour course as well as a much larger 1800-hour program. He says the longer program runs for about eight months, with intensive daily classes and a faculty team supporting the training.

The larger message is clear. A yoga career should not be built only around a certificate. It should be built around knowledge, practice, teaching ability, discipline and the ability to work responsibly with students. The goal is not simply to complete a course. The goal is to become capable of teaching and continuing to learn. That certificate-versus-capability tension also runs through N.P. Singh on degrees vs skill.

Yoga Teacher Training: Why Practice Matters More Than a Certificate

One of the strongest themes in the episode is the difference between qualification and capability.

A certificate can show that someone has completed a course, but it cannot automatically demonstrate teaching maturity. Yogacharya Dhakaram stresses the importance of practice and experience. A teacher should continue learning and practising rather than treating certification as the end of the journey.

This is especially relevant as interest in yoga teacher training in India grows. Aspiring teachers have many options, including foundational courses and advanced programs.

The practical lesson is simple: when choosing a yoga teacher training program, look beyond the number printed on the certificate. Examine the depth of curriculum, faculty experience, practice teaching, discipline, assessment and opportunities to develop real teaching skills.

What Career Opportunities Are Available After Yoga Teacher Training?

A trained yoga professional can explore different directions depending on skills, experience and specialisation. These may include teaching at yoga studios, wellness centres, fitness spaces, schools, institutions, corporate wellness programs, retreats and online platforms. Experienced professionals can also build private teaching practices, workshops or specialised wellness services.

The episode places strong emphasis on the idea that yoga can become a livelihood. This creates an important shift in thinking. A yoga professional can move from being only a job seeker to becoming a teacher, trainer, consultant, studio owner or wellness entrepreneur.

The opportunity, however, comes with responsibility. A sustainable yoga business depends on trust, quality teaching, student experience, professional behaviour and continuous development. Browse more guests in the guest index or the full writings archive.

Can Yoga Become a Business?

Yes, but the business opportunity is closely connected to professional credibility.

The conversation explores whether yoga is only a health practice or whether it can also become a business model that creates employment and independent opportunities. Yogacharya Dhakaram strongly supports the idea that yoga can provide a livelihood.

A yoga business can take different forms. It may involve personal teaching, group classes, workshops, teacher training, wellness programs, specialised sessions, retreats or digital education.

The strongest businesses are likely to be those that solve a clear audience need. For example, a teacher may focus on beginners, working professionals, older adults, children, corporate wellness or specialised yoga education. Why Experience and Discipline Matter in Yoga

The episode repeatedly returns to discipline and the responsibility of a teacher.

Yoga teaching is not only about demonstrating a posture. It involves understanding students, communicating clearly, maintaining professional boundaries and continuing personal practice.

Dhakaram also discusses institutional discipline, student development and the importance of experience. His approach suggests that teachers should not stop learning after receiving a qualification.

This is particularly important for people entering the yoga profession because the market can include teachers with very different levels of training and experience.

Therapeutic Yoga and the Growing Wellness Conversation

Another major topic in the episode is Therapeutic Yoga.

Yogacharya Dhakaram describes a holistic approach in which yoga is used in a therapeutic context. He discusses his experience working with people dealing with different health concerns and argues that the value of therapeutic yoga is likely to increase as interest in wellness grows.

These statements should be understood as the guest's experience and perspective rather than as a medical guarantee. Yoga should not automatically be presented as a replacement for medical diagnosis or treatment.

For aspiring professionals, however, the discussion highlights a career direction. Specialised knowledge can differentiate a yoga teacher from a general instructor. Training in areas such as therapeutic applications, anatomy, safe practice and population-specific teaching can help professionals work more responsibly. What Does the Future of Yoga Look Like?

The future of yoga is likely to extend beyond traditional studio classes.

The conversation points toward a wider ecosystem involving education, professional training, wellness services and specialised teaching. As more people look for structured approaches to physical and mental wellbeing, qualified yoga professionals can potentially serve audiences in India and internationally.

At the same time, growth creates competition. More people entering the field means that credibility, practical experience and specialisation become increasingly important.

The future therefore may belong not simply to those who know yoga, but to those who can teach it responsibly, communicate its value clearly and continue developing their expertise.

What Should You Consider Before Starting a Yoga Career?

Before enrolling in a yoga teacher training program, ask yourself five questions.

  1. Do I want yoga to remain a personal practice or become my profession?
  2. Am I prepared for regular practice and continuous learning?
  3. Does the training program provide meaningful practical teaching experience?
  4. Do I want to specialise in a particular audience or area?
  5. Can I build a professional identity based on trust and quality rather than only social media visibility?

Key Takeaways

  1. Yoga can become a meaningful career when approached with discipline, training and continuous practice.
  2. A certificate is useful, but practical teaching ability and experience matter greatly.
  3. Yoga careers can include teaching, wellness programs, workshops, training, private practice and entrepreneurship.
  4. A successful yoga business should solve a clear audience need and build long-term trust.
  5. Therapeutic yoga can be an important area of specialisation, but health claims should always be handled responsibly.
  6. The future of yoga is likely to include education, wellness, specialised services and global opportunities.
  7. The strongest professional advantage is not a shortcut. It is depth of knowledge, practice and the ability to serve students responsibly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is yoga a good career in India?
Yoga can be a viable career path for people who develop strong teaching skills, professional credibility and practical experience. Career options can include studios, wellness centres, schools, corporate programs, private teaching and online education.
How can I become a yoga teacher?
Start with a consistent personal practice, choose a suitable yoga teacher training program, develop theoretical and practical knowledge, complete the required training and gain supervised teaching experience.
Is yoga teacher training worth it?
Quality training can provide structured knowledge, teaching methodology and practical experience. The value depends heavily on the curriculum, faculty, practice opportunities and credibility of the institution.
Can I start a yoga business?
Yes. A yoga business can include personal classes, group programs, workshops, retreats, corporate wellness, teacher training and online services. Sustainable growth depends on expertise, trust and a clear audience need.
Is a yoga certificate enough to become a successful teacher?
No. A certificate is only one part of professional development. Regular practice, teaching experience, communication, discipline and continued education are essential.
What is the future of yoga as a profession?
The future looks broader than traditional studio teaching. Opportunities may expand across wellness, education, corporate programs, specialised instruction, online learning and international audiences.
Is therapeutic yoga a medical treatment?
Therapeutic yoga may be used as a complementary wellness or therapeutic approach, but it should not be presented as a guaranteed replacement for medical care. Health conditions require appropriate professional medical guidance.
