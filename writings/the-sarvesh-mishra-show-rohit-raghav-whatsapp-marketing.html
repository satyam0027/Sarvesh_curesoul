WhatsApp Business App vs API

Business AppBusiness API
CostFreePaid per message through a provider
Best forSolo owners, local shopsScale, automation, chatbots
Team accessOne deviceMany agents
Bulk sendingLimitedYes, with approved templates

Start with the app if you have a small list. Move to the API when you need automation, many agents or large campaigns.

SMS vs WhatsApp vs RCS

ChannelDelivery (his figures)InteractionBest use
SMSAbout 99.9 percentOne wayOTPs, bills, alerts
WhatsAppAbout 80 to 90 percentTwo way, richOffers, chatbots, invoices

He says WhatsApp gets opened most because people check it dozens of times a day, while SMS reaches almost every active number.

Cost per message

Message typePrice
SMS, partner price (his figure)About 9 paise plus GST
WhatsApp utility (his figure)About 13 to 15 paise
WhatsApp marketing (a competitor guide’s figure)Roughly ₹0.86

Prices change and depend on message category, so check current Meta and provider rates.

His lead cost example: 1,000 messages at 15 paise cost ₹150. If 10 people reply, each enquiry costs about ₹15, while a telecaller may spend a day on 200 calls with no result.

The fallback flow

A common problem is managing failed messages by hand. His fix:

  1. Send on WhatsApp first.
  2. Failed messages go out on RCS.
  3. Remaining failures go out as SMS.

This saves staff time and wasted spend.

IVR for business

IVR routes incoming calls to many agents at once and logs every caller. Without it, one phone handles one call, so leads from a big campaign can be missed. He quotes about ₹12,000 to ₹13,000 a year.

His main point: reaching your existing customers again is cheaper than finding new ones.

Rules: consent, templates and opt out

WhatsApp is governed mainly by Meta’s own business messaging policy, while SMS follows TRAI’s separate framework. The common principle is permission first.

RuleIn practice
Opt inMessage only people who agreed, and keep a record
Approved templatesBusiness-started messages use approved templates
Clear senderUse a verified business profile
Easy opt outHonour stop requests quickly
Relevant contentMatch messages to what people agreed to receive
QualityComplaints and blocks can lower your quality rating and limits

Bought or random lists are the biggest risk. They can lead to restrictions, poor delivery and legal exposure. For your specific case, check Meta’s current policy, TRAI rules and India’s data protection law, or ask a qualified advisor.

How to start: a 30-day test

WeekAction
1Clean your own permission-based list and pick one goal
2Compare two verified providers and send 100 test messages
3Add a simple chatbot and call tracking, then measure cost per enquiry
4Improve the offer and timing, then scale only what works

Reality check

  • The market size figures in the transcript were inconsistent, so they are not used here.
  • The claim of a partner earning ₹1 crore in eight months is one reported result, not typical.
  • If you plan to resell, ask for a trial, written terms and references from current partners. The guest mentioned plans from ₹9,999, and these are his terms.
  • The guest says AI helps with support and content, but buyers of high-value items still want to meet people.
Key takeaways
  • Use the Business app for small lists and the API for scale.
  • SMS gives reach, WhatsApp gives engagement and RCS fits Android branding.
  • A fallback flow cuts wasted spend.
  • Existing customers are your cheapest audience.
  • IVR stops lead loss.
  • Get consent first and honour opt outs.
  • Verify income claims before paying for any package.

Conclusion

WhatsApp marketing in India works when you treat it as a permission-first system, not a blast. Know your audience, test a small batch, track the cost of each enquiry and make sure no lead is lost. Use WhatsApp for engagement, SMS for reach and a simple chatbot and IVR for the first reply. Start with a 30-day test and scale only what earns.

Frequently asked questions

What is WhatsApp marketing in India?

Using the Business app or API to send offers, reminders and invoices, and to chat with customers.

What is the difference between the Business app and API?

The app is free for small use. The API is paid and built for automation, chatbots and teams.

How much does a WhatsApp message cost in India?

The guest quoted about 13 to 15 paise for utility messages. Marketing messages cost more, so check current rates.

Which is better, SMS, WhatsApp or RCS?

WhatsApp for engagement, SMS for reach and OTPs, RCS for rich messages on Android.

Is bulk WhatsApp marketing legal?

Yes, if people have opted in and you use approved templates.

Do TRAI rules apply to WhatsApp?

WhatsApp is governed mainly by Meta’s policies, while TRAI’s rules cover SMS and calls. The shared principles are consent, honoured preferences and a clear sender.

Can I message any number?

No. Message only people who gave permission. Bought lists risk complaints and restrictions.

What is IVR for business?

A system that routes calls to many agents and logs every lead.

Can I resell WhatsApp services?

The guest says yes. Ask for a trial, written terms and references.

How do I get better conversions?

Target the right list, test timing and offers, and measure cost per enquiry.
