The Inner Wealth · Day 3 of 7 · Guest: Anurag Rishi

Understanding Aura, Energy & Life Decisions — Questions & Answers

Watch the episode on YouTube: open Day 3 video. Host: Sarvesh Mishra.

In three lines

  • Decisions and “failure” carry emotional energy — setbacks sometimes reroute us toward something truer.
  • Aura is framed as the body–mind energetic field: thought, feeling, and nervous-system activity woven together.
  • Coherence beats slogans — affirmations and calm strengthen when thought, emotion, and practice align.

Structured Q&A aligned with this Inner Wealth episode. Not medical or personal counselling.

Questions & answers

Q1. Why do some decisions fail even when we think carefully before making them?

Sometimes what appears to be a failure is actually life redirecting us toward something better. A closed door may feel painful in the moment, but later we often realize it protected us or guided us toward a more meaningful path.

Q2. Can failure actually become a blessing?

Yes. Many experiences that initially feel like failure later become turning points in life. What looks like loss today may create space for growth, healing, purpose, or a completely new opportunity tomorrow.

Q3. Do emotions affect our decisions?

Absolutely. Decisions made in anger, fear, love, happiness, pain, or betrayal carry different energies. Often, our emotional state influences our thinking more than logic itself.

Q4. What is “aura”?

Aura is believed to be an energetic field surrounding the human body. In spiritual traditions, it is called the “energy field” or “aura field,” while modern science sometimes relates it to bio-energy or electromagnetic activity generated by the body and mind.

Q5. Is aura a real concept or just a trend?

The conversation explains that aura is connected to the body’s energy, emotions, thoughts, and nervous system. While spiritual traditions have discussed it for centuries, modern science is now exploring similar concepts through bioelectricity, electromagnetic fields, and energy studies.

Q6. How is aura connected to thoughts and emotions?

Thoughts create electrical impulses in the brain, while emotions and feelings create magnetic responses within the body and heart. Together, these form an electromagnetic field, which is described as the aura.

Q7. Why do we instantly feel comfortable or uncomfortable around certain people?

According to the discussion, energy fields interact before words are even spoken. Sometimes a person’s presence immediately makes us feel calm, happy, heavy, anxious, or uncomfortable. This reaction is believed to happen through energetic interaction or intuition.

Q8. Can our body sense energy before the mind understands it?

Yes. The body often reacts before conscious thought begins. You may suddenly feel tension, relaxation, attraction, or discomfort around someone without logically knowing why. The conversation describes this as the body and energy field responding before the conscious mind catches up.

Q9. What creates a strong aura?

A strong aura is believed to come from emotional balance, clarity of thought, positive feelings, inner peace, gratitude, love, and alignment between mind and heart. When thoughts, emotions, and feelings work together in harmony, energy becomes stronger and more stable.

Q10. Can negative emotions weaken our aura?

Yes. Fear, guilt, shame, jealousy, anger, stress, and unresolved emotional pain can weaken emotional energy and affect the body’s energetic balance.

Q11. What is emotional coherence?

Emotional coherence means your thoughts, emotions, and feelings are aligned with each other. For example, if you think positively and genuinely feel those emotions deeply, your energy becomes stronger and more stable.

Q12. Why do affirmations sometimes fail to work?

Affirmations work only when emotions and feelings support them. Repeating positive sentences without emotionally believing or feeling them creates only mental repetition, not energetic transformation.

Q13. Can meditation and spiritual practices improve aura?

Yes. The conversation explains that practices like meditation, breathwork, chakra-focused awareness, gratitude, and emotional healing can strengthen emotional balance and expand a person’s energetic field.

Q14. Why do some places or people drain our energy?

Every environment carries emotional and energetic influence. Hospitals, stressful environments, arguments, or emotionally heavy people can sometimes leave us mentally exhausted. Similarly, peaceful places and positive people can make us feel lighter and energized.

Q15. Can intuition really guide us?

The discussion suggests that intuition is deeply connected to energy awareness. Sometimes we “just know” when something feels right or wrong before logic explains it. This inner sensing may come from subconscious emotional processing and energetic interaction.

Q16. How can someone improve their energy and emotional state?

Some suggested practices include:

  • Meditation
  • Deep breathing
  • Emotional healing
  • Gratitude
  • Self-awareness
  • Positive relationships
  • Inner reflection
  • Letting go of emotional baggage

These practices help create emotional balance and mental clarity.

Q17. What is the deeper message behind these teachings?

The deeper message is that humans are not only physical beings but also emotional, mental, and energetic beings. Understanding ourselves beyond surface-level thinking may help us live with more awareness, peace, and clarity.
