Wishing Will Not Make You Rich: How to Quit Your Job and Start a Business in 2026

Almost everyone who earns a salary dreams of financial freedom and becoming a crorepati. Yet most hardworking people in India stay financially stuck. The reason is rarely a lack of effort. It is a lack of direction. On The Sarvesh Mishra Show, Sarvesh Mishra asks Sunil Chopra how to quit your job and start a business in 2026 — in the right order: know where you stand, follow one clear formula and stay patient.

Why hard work alone does not build wealth

Sunil Chopra explains this with a balloon. Fill it with air and let it go, and it has plenty of energy but no destination. A missile has the same energy, yet it knows where it is and where it must land.

Your effort works the same way. Your first step is to know your starting line. What skills do you have today, what are your gaps and how much have you saved? Your dream can be as big as you like, but your first step must come from reality.

How your entrepreneur mindset shapes your business

Many people hear “you are useless” from childhood, and that voice settles into their thinking. Chopra suggests replacing it with a goal focused mindset. Write your goal, repeat it daily and act on it. This is his personal view and not a guarantee. The real change comes when a clear goal is backed by daily action.

Are you ready to quit your job?

Ask yourself why you work. If it is for your family, EMIs and responsibilities, that is a clear and valid reason. If you cannot tell who you are working for or why, that may be the first sign you want to build something of your own.

Another sign is salary day. If the joy lasts one day and the other 30 days feel like a grind, think about what you really want. Even then, do not resign the morning after watching a podcast. Start a side hustle first, test your idea and then decide.

The 5 One formula to start a business

You do not need a giant idea or a huge fund on day one. You need focus. This is also the simplest answer to how to start a business with no money.

  1. One market: pick a specific group, such as students in one city or small shop owners.
  2. One problem: find a real problem that group faces again and again.
  3. One offer: decide what you can create or sell to solve that problem.
  4. One platform: choose where you will reach customers, such as your own website, Instagram, LinkedIn or a marketplace.
  5. One year: commit seriously for twelve months. Most people quit before the market has had time to respond.

Entrepreneurship means more responsibility

Many people believe business means doing things their own way with nobody to answer to. The truth is the opposite. In a job, your salary arrives on the 7th. In a business, you pay salaries, bills, EMIs and taxes first. Winning your first 10 customers can take longer than you expect. Consistency beats mood every time.

Why most businesses fail to scale

According to Chopra, the biggest trap is founder attachment. The founder treats the business like a child and never lets it grow. Sales, collections and every decision stay in one pair of hands, so the company stays stuck at 5 crore, 10 crore or 50 crore.

The fix is to hire good people, give them room to work and delegate step by step. Keep only the critical decisions with you. Good people leave when they cannot make an impact.

Investors back founders first

Money is an enabler, not the engine. Investors bet on the founder more than the business. They study your thinking, your background and your team. This is why personal branding for founders matters. When customers and investors connect with your story, trust grows and good people, sales and funding follow.

Asset light business model: do more with less

Asset light growth means shifting your focus from money and headcount to resourcefulness. How can a team of five deliver the work of fifty? Use your network, partners and part time talent. Chopra began with a list of 63 people he had worked with before and built from there.

Build one good habit in 2026

Indians spend about 194 minutes a day on social media, which adds up to roughly 70,000 minutes a year. Redirect a small part of that time to your growth:

  • Keep your tool nearby, such as a notebook, a book or a gym bag.
  • Give it just 10 minutes a day.
  • Reward yourself when you finish.

Finally, be the hero of your own film. Do not try to play a role written for someone else.

Common mistakes to avoid before you quit

  • Resigning on impulse after watching motivational content
  • Starting without a clear market or problem
  • Believing business means freedom instead of responsibility
  • Waiting for a perfect idea or a large fund
  • Giving up before completing a full year
  • Keeping every decision with the founder after the business starts to grow

Your 30 day action plan

This plan is a practical way to apply the episode. It is not part of the guest’s advice.

  1. Week 1: know your starting line. Write down your skills, gaps, savings and monthly obligations. Write your purpose for working in one sentence.
  2. Week 2: pick your market and problem. Choose one group and list the three biggest problems they face.
  3. Week 3: design one offer. Decide what you can sell and where you will reach customers. Pick one platform only.
  4. Week 4: test on the side. Talk to 10 potential customers, make your first offer and note what they say. Start a daily 10 minute habit that supports your goal.
Key takeaways
  • Direction matters more than effort. Know your starting line before you quit.
  • Use the 5 One formula: one market, one problem, one offer, one platform, one year.
  • Test as a side hustle. Do not resign on impulse after a podcast.
  • Business means more responsibility, not more freedom.
  • Scale by delegating. Founder attachment keeps companies stuck.
  • Investors back founders first. Personal branding builds trust.
  • No formula can promise you will become a crorepati.

Frequently asked questions

How do I quit my job and start a business in 2026?

Clarify your purpose and finances first. Then use the 5 One formula to build one small offer and test it as a side hustle before you resign.

Do I need a lot of money to start a business?

No. You can begin small with one market, one problem and one offer, and grow as customers arrive.

What are the signs I am ready to start a business?

You have a clear reason for leaving, a real problem you can solve, some savings to cover your obligations and early interest from potential customers.

Should I start a side hustle before quitting my job?

Yes, in most cases. A side hustle lets you test demand and earn your first customers without risking your monthly income.

Is a formal degree necessary for business?

Not always. In most businesses, knowledge, skill and practice in your chosen field matter more than a degree.

Why do businesses fail to scale?

Usually because the founder keeps every task and decision. Delegation, systems and a strong team unlock growth.

What is the 5 One formula?

It is a focus framework shared by Sunil Chopra: one market, one problem, one offer, one platform and one year of serious effort.

What is an asset light business model?

It means growing through resourcefulness, such as networks, partnerships and part time talent, instead of heavy spending on money, space and headcount.

Why does personal branding matter for founders?

Investors, customers and good hires back people they trust. A clear, honest founder story builds that trust faster than advertising.

Is entrepreneurship better than a job?

Neither is better for everyone. A job gives stability and a fixed salary. A business offers upside but demands more responsibility and patience.

How can I contact Sunil Chopra?

He says he is available through his platform YourWinCoach, LinkedIn and Instagram. He offers a two hour discovery session, which he describes as a paid engagement.

Can anyone really become a crorepati?

No formula can promise it, but clear direction, daily action and patience raise your chances a great deal.

Watch the full conversation

Wanting wealth is not enough. In this episode, host Sarvesh Mishra and guest Sunil Chopra make one point clear: direction matters more than effort. Know your starting point, choose one market and one problem, give it a full year and let your team grow. Build a personal brand that people trust and add one good habit to your day. When your direction is right, your results will follow.
