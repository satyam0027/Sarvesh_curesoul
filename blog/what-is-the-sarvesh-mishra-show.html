The Sarvesh Mishra Show

What Is The Sarvesh Mishra Show?

The Sarvesh Mishra Show is a long-form YouTube podcast hosted by Sarvesh Mishra — a journalist, interviewer, media entrepreneur, and former field reporter. The show features unscripted, in-depth conversations with entrepreneurs, authors, leaders, and thinkers. Unlike most podcast formats, The Sarvesh Mishra Show goes beyond career stories — each guest conversation goes into how they think, lead, and live.

About The Sarvesh Mishra Show

The Sarvesh Mishra Show is Sarvesh Mishra's flagship YouTube podcast. It began from the same discipline that shaped his journalism — the belief that understanding how people think is more valuable than collecting what they have achieved. The show is recorded at ES Podcast Studio, Noida. Each episode runs long-form — typically sixty to ninety minutes — because that is how long it takes to get past the rehearsed answers.

Topics include leadership, entrepreneurship, identity crises, decision-making under pressure, inner clarity, and modern stress. The show has no fixed script. Sarvesh Mishra asks the questions no one else thinks to ask — the ones that reveal pattern, pressure, and the private logic behind public success.

What Topics Does The Sarvesh Mishra Show Cover?

Leadership and decision-making under real pressure

Episodes examine how leaders behave when stakes are high — not the polished version from a stage, but the decisions made when information is incomplete, reputations are on the line, and there is no script to follow.

Entrepreneurship — the inner cost, not just the outer success

Founders speak about what building actually required: doubt, debt, broken relationships, delayed gratification, and the identity shifts that come with scaling a company or a mission.

Identity — who you are when roles are stripped away

Many guests arrive with titles. The conversation often reaches the person underneath — what remains when the designation, the company, or the public image is removed.

Emotional patterns — fear, ambition, attachment, stress

The show treats emotion as data, not drama. Fear of failure, attachment to outcomes, chronic stress, and the ambition that both drives and distorts are named clearly and explored without performance.

Conscious living — spiritual depth applied to modern work life

Guests who carry spiritual or philosophical depth do not leave it at the door. The Sarvesh Mishra Show asks how that depth shows up in business, family, health, and daily decision-making.

How Sarvesh Mishra reads a life

Across episodes, Sarvesh Mishra listens for structure: how a person interprets pressure, what they repeat under stress, and what their choices reveal about their inner architecture. That lens is what makes the show distinct.

Who Are the Guests on The Sarvesh Mishra Show?

The show hosts founders, CEOs, authors, coaches, doctors, journalists, and public figures — but with one filter. The guest must have something real to say. Not a brand to sell, not a book to promote. The Sarvesh Mishra Show selects guests who have lived through pressure, built something meaningful, or developed a point of view that can genuinely help a listener decode their own life.

How Is The Sarvesh Mishra Show Different from Other Podcasts?

Most podcasts chase inspiration. The Sarvesh Mishra Show chases understanding. Sarvesh Mishra's background as a journalist who covered disasters, courts, villages, and human crises gives him a quality most podcast hosts do not have — he knows when someone is performing and when they are being real. The result is conversations that go inward, not upward.
