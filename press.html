Official short bio

For use in introductions, event programs, and article references.

Sarvesh Mishra is an Indian entrepreneur, journalist, interviewer, and storyteller. He is the founder of Red Hot Media House Pvt. Ltd. (media & production) and CureSoulLife Pvt. Ltd. (conscious living & wellness). He hosts three long-form media properties — The Sarvesh Mishra Show, The Inner Wealth, and The Urban Sannyasi — across dedicated YouTube channels.

With nearly 15 years in journalism and on-ground reporting across 11 Indian states — including covering the 2013 Uttarakhand disaster — Sarvesh brings a rare combination of field-level depth, narrative discipline, and entrepreneurial vision to everything he builds.
